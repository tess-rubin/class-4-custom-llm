# Added-corpus review

Codex composed both files before reading the 48 suite stories. Each file has 120 separately written, single-sentence teaching passages; the loader produces exactly 120 unique passages from each, with no extraction warnings. Every sentence is below the 47-token cap. There are no downloaded texts, test stories, answer lists, or generated model outputs in these files. The user selected opposites and negation and authorized AI-written explanations and continued execution.

Opposites span size, fullness, weight, taste, width, length, cleanliness, moisture, time, speed, openness, texture, brightness, height, distance, age, sound, temperature, and strength. Negation varies not/no/never, neither/nor, without, inability, rejection, exclusions, and qualified claims. The situations use ordinary classroom, market, transport, household, and health contexts. These are synthetic language lessons, not practical medical or financial advice.

After authoring, all 240 passages and all suite cases were reviewed. The added texts do not reproduce the suite's color-box story, named purchaser story, door-state recap, named reference stories, relation-restatement questions, or explicit opposite-question template. Generic concepts such as full/empty, open/closed, and bright/dark intentionally overlap; sharing a concept is the purpose of teaching examples. Exact-prefix checks pass, and manual review found no close story paraphrases. This is an evidence-based review, not an automated proof of semantic separation.

The expanded run retains 240 unique additions after normalizing and deduplicating against the base. Their UTF-8 source hashes, previews, extracted counts, warning lists, split assignments, and vocabulary omissions remain in the linked manifests. It retains 509 of 562 training word/punctuation types. The training and validation token unknown rates are 0.10677% and 0.74289%. The term `opposite` itself was never written into the added prose, so contrast examples did not give the model that token; the opposites tests remain unscorable. No passages were changed to improve test scores after observing the results.
